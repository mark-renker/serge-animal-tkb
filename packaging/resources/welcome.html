A software recreation of a Serge-style modular voice: the "Animal" system (New Timbral Oscillator, Wave Multipliers, Ring Modulator, Variable Q VCF, noise and random voltages, slope generators, smooth & stepped, dual VCA, output mixer) plus the TKB touch-activated keyboard sequencer.

On the next screens you can choose which formats to install (Audio Unit, VST3 and, where available, the standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
